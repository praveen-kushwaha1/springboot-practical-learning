## How to use this lab

1. **Read the objective** before opening the code.
2. **Run the starter example** and observe the output.
3. **Change something intentionally** and predict what will happen.
4. **Complete the exercise** without copying a solution first.
5. **Write tests** and verify both success and failure cases.
6. **Commit your work** with a clear message.

<div class="callout-strip">
  <strong>Learning rule:</strong> Don't just read a topic. Implement it, test it, explain it in your own words, and commit it.
</div>
